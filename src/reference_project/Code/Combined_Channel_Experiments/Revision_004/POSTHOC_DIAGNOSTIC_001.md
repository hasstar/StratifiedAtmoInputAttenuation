# Post-result zero-attenuation diagnostic (not a selected/fitted model)

After observing the approximate physical results and their negative overall R2,
check a fixed zero-dB prediction on the identical samples. This check is explicitly
post hoc and separate from the preregistered 18 physical scenarios /20 saved runs.
Zero is a physical no-excess-attenuation reference, not a mean estimated from UK
labels, a calibrated offset or a model selected from alternative constants.
Report its point metrics descriptively to avoid claiming that a small absolute
RMSE alone establishes useful UK signal reconstruction. No new significance test,
hyperparameter choice, fit, ML inference or modification of primary tables.
