## Interpretation policy

Common inputs do not erase architecture/hyperparameter-search differences. Rev029 was developed through previous search/RFE; these are bounded new comparator runs, not identical total search budgets. Report this history alongside seed variability. Do not compare Q-band published error numbers directly with our19.13/20.7GHz errors. External paired stations share meteorology and are not independent climate replications. No result in this register asserts new blind/temporal generalization or completion of full fast-channel comparison.
